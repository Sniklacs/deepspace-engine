**Concept under validation:** persistent-world colony-management survival; post-war human-vs-AI planet; salvage AI fragments from ruin "Shatterlands," study them in labs, rebuild/rebuild-toward-your-own AI workforce; AI is both salvation and threat; optional multiplayer (guilds/co-op or competition); cosmetics monetization.

## Q1 — GENRE LANDSCAPE (persistent-world / survival / strategy)
1. **Rust** — open-world PvP survival. ~100k+ daily peak, spiking 200k+ on content drops/free weekends `[approx, verify]`. Pull: harsh loss progression, full-loot PvP, wipe cycle.
2. **Minecraft** — tens of millions; the persistent-world template, not a strategy comp.
3. **Palworld** (Jan 2024) — survival + collection; ~2.1M Steam concurrent peak `[approx, verify]`. Appetite for accessible survival with a novel hook.
4. **Albion Online** — closest "Eve-lite": single-server, player economy, full-loot PvP. ~100k+ Steam concurrent `[approx, verify]`. Studio-scale (Sandbox Interactive, ~100+ staff, ~10 yrs, custom non-browser client).
5. **Eve Online** — single-shard persistent space MMO, sub-based. ~20–30k avg concurrent, ~500k+ active accounts `[approx, verify]`. The archetype asked about.
6. **Once Human** (Jul 2024) — free survival; ~230k+ launch peak `[approx, verify]`. Post-apoc/supernatural still converts.
7. **Satisfactory**(1.0 Sep 2024)/**Factorio** — automation/logistics. ~100k+ at 1.0; Factorio 3.5M+ copies, ~20–40k concurrent `[approx, verify]`.
8. **Stellaris** — space 4X. ~15–30k concurrent, millions of owners `[approx, verify]`. Includes playable gestalt/AI empires — direct AI-as-mechanic analogue.
9. **Foxhole** — persistent player-driven war, logistics as gameplay. ~3–8k concurrent `[approx, verify]`. Proves a *narrow* persistent concept sustains a dedicated community at indie-ish scale.
10. **Zomboid / Kenshi / RimWorld / Frostpunk** — colony/survival (mostly SP/co-op). Zomboid ~30–40k, 5M+ copies; RimWorld 5M+; Frostpunk 2 ~350k week-one `[approx, verify]`. Strongest direct comps for the concept's core loop.

**Read:** two poles — action/social survival (Rust, Valheim, Palworld) and management/logistics depth (RimWorld, Factorio, Stellaris, Foxhole). The concept sits in the **under-built middle**: persistent-online *colony/management* survival.

## Q2 — THEME TRENDS
- **Post-apocalyptic is evergreen & surging** (2024): Fallout TV re-spiked F76 to ~100k+; Stalker 2 ~100k; Frostpunk 2 ~350k; Once Human; Dying Light. "World ended, rebuild/scavenge ruins" is a proven frame.
- **AI-as-theme** at all-time cultural high, but in games mostly narrative skin (Detroit, Talos Principle 2, SOMA, NieR) or robot enemies.
- **AI-as-mechanic is rare**: Stellaris (gestalt/AI empires), Caves of Qud (procedural post-apoc robots, 1.0 Dec 2024), Factorio/Satisfactory (automation), RimWorld (mechanoids). **No mainstream title makes "salvage fragments of rival intelligence, study, rebuild workforce/weaponry AI" the central loop — largely open real estate.**
- **Human-vs-machine is strong**: Helldivers 2 (~450k peak) made its machine faction a beloved antagonist; players respond to "we created the machines" grayness.

## Q3 — THE EVE-LIKE QUESTION (honest)
**A true Eve-like single-shard MMO is NOT viable indie/browser; a smaller persistent-world survival/strategy game is the stronger, only realistic position.**
- Single-shard requires 24/7 authoritative sim, scaling, cheat mitigation, cross-player economy. Eve ~7 yrs + large studio; every comp is studio-built. **No successful indie browser single-shard MMO precedent.**
- Indie/free titles win on co-op, async persistence, or per-community servers.
- Browser client caps authoritative concurrency; small-instance/async/zone persistence is far more tractable.
- **Constructive replacement:** a few dozen to low-hundreds of players per world/server, co-op-first with optional territory contention, world persistence (colony/economy survive offline). Core is **PvE vs AI hives** — much more indie-friendly than Eve's PvP single-shard.

## Q4 — POSITIONING GAP
**Surface crowded; specific position not.** "Persistent-online colony/management survival" is the gap — strategically deep (management, economy, logistics, territory, consequential peaceful progression) where colony/economy persists for days/weeks. Foxhole proves strategic persistence appetite; RimWorld/Frostpunk prove management depth; nobody dominant pairs the two online.
- **Unserved asks:** (1) persistence that *means* something; (2) logistics/economy/automation as core, not a chore; (3) moral ambiguity/narrative stakes (AI = savior AND threat).
- **AI-fragment-as-mechanic is fresh** (see Q2).
- **Honest caveat:** management/sim audience is smaller but far more loyal; must be executed with real systems depth, and requires distinctive visual identity to beat genre-crowding.

## Q5 — AUDIENCE
- **Primary:** colony-sim + 4X + automation/logistics players (RimWorld, Frostpunk, Kenshi, Zomboid, Stellaris, Factorio, Satisfactory). 25–45, PC-first, systems-driven, value depth. Loyal niche (RimWorld ~5M+, Factorio ~3.5M+, Frostpunk/Stellaris millions) `[approx, verify]`.
- **Secondary:** persistent-online survival players wanting strategic tilt over twitch PvP (Eve/Albion-curious, Foxhole logistics).
- **Novelty fringe:** AI-curious hype crowd — launch buzz only, not a retention bet.
- **Realistic launch audience:** tens of thousands (wishlists + community word-of-mouth), growing organically.
- **Receptive communities:** r/RimWorld, r/factorio, r/SatisfactoryGame, r/projectzomboid, r/Kenshi, r/Stellaris, r/Eve, r/albiononline, colony-sim & indie-dev communities.

## RECOMMENDATION
**Should we build it? — Yes, but repositioned.** It occupies an under-served intersection (online + colony-management + AI-as-economic-mechanic + post-war rebuild), is indie-feasible if scope-controlled, and its core tension (AI = salvation AND threat) is a strong gameplay loop and marketing hook.

**Single most important refinement:** **Drop the "Eve-like single-shard MMO" framing entirely; reposition as a persistent co-op colony-management survival game** — "rebuild your colony with the AI you salvage; survive and contest territory against hostile AI hives." Multiplayer: optional co-op/guilds on per-world servers (a few dozen players), consent-based or territory-contention PvP at most, fully-supported SP/co-op as baseline. Keeps the distinctive AI-fragment hook, is buildable by an indie team + browser client, targets the underserved strategic-management niche.

**Secondary refinements:** make the **AI-fragment study/rebuild loop the first system** (it *is* the identity); emphasize **meaningful long-term persistence** (colony + economy survive offline) as the differentiator; invest in **distinctive visual identity** early.
