## Concept
The War Room is a BIG HALLWAY of chalkboards. The walls carry HANDWRITTEN game tips —
chalkboard style, not formal text blocks. The tips ARE the game's FAQ, written as riddles
the way the Oracle would say them: cryptic one-liners, no explanation, no questions —
just offhand comments scrawled on the wall, like someone wrote them and walked away.
Hans named them OFF THE WALL.

## Rules
- Tips are cryptic one-liners with NO explanation (e.g. "I hope you have that elite squad
  at home waiting, ready to roll if shit hits the fan and your ass needs carrying out of there").
  They click AFTER the player learns the hard way.
- This is WHY the war room exists: players discover the walls after getting burned,
  then start reading them.
- The battle doctrine maxims ("casualties = commitment," etc.) live on these walls too,
  alongside playbook diagrams and X's-and-O's — the chalkboard IS the War Room skin.
- Tips are SMALL on the wall — almost hard to read, part of the texture.
  Tap one → it magnifies, pulls up big so you can read it.
- Interactive wall: tips, plays, formations, doctrines — one overwhelming hallway.
