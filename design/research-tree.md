# Research Tree — RESOURCE SEPARATION (owner decision 2026-09-01, authoritative)

## The two-track resource model
- **Embers & Chipsets = AI fragments** — the *tool* track. Salvaged in expedition, studied/deployed to advance the colony's domains (weaponry, agriculture, economy, industry, logistics). This is "AI as instrument and threat."
- **RESEARCH runs on a DIFFERENT, SPECIAL collectible** — NOT embers/chipsets. The research tree (understanding / new technology) is fed by a distinct scarce resource that is morally and mechanically separate from AI fragments.

## What this resource is (theme)
The one thing the war/AI nearly erased: **human knowledge** — pre-war texts, journals, records, science, citizen history. It's special and rare precisely because the Chorus drowned it out. Collecting it is the act of reclaiming what it means to be human in a world rebuilt on AI parts.

## Proposed name (choose one)
- **Codices** — old human bound-knowledge (recommended; evokes manuscript/record, contrasts with AI's "chips")
- **The Archive / Archive folios** — institutionalized human record
- **Memories / Recollection** — poetic, ties to loss
- **Relics** — human heritage artifacts

## Mechanical consequence
- Expedition loop now has (at least) two distinct loot streams: AI fragments (feed deploy/domains) AND this special research collectible (feeds the research tree).
- The research collectible comes from places/conditions where pre-war human knowledge survived — likely overlapping deep/special sites, but as a SEPARATE yield line (not the same drop as embers/chipsets).
- Research tree nodes cost this special resource (plus maybe time/insight), NOT embers/chipsets.
- Strong thematic tie: this is the human counter-track to the AI track, and it dovetails with the Unbound (rebuild-without-AI) path and with Leaders (named humans who personify that knowledge).

## HOW CODICES ARE ACQUIRED (owner decision 2026-09-01, authoritative)
**EARNED-first, with rare discovery — NOT a loot grind.**
- Primary: earned through MEANINGFUL OUTCOMES, not random salvage — rescuing a surviving archive/record, completing a specific recovery objective, protecting a scholar/site, deeds/awards of merit.
- Secondary: occasionally FOUND in exploration as a rare discovery (bonus, not the volume source).
- Rationale: embers you SALVAGE, chipsets you RAID for, Codices you EARN. Keeps the scarce resource special and mirrors the Unbound track (earned, not looted). The special things in the game are earned.
- Name confirmed by owner: **CODICES** (Cody + owner liked it).

## RESEARCH RUNS ON APPOINTED LEADERS (Cody's idea, owner-endorsed 2026-09-01)
- To run/research a research-tree project, you **APPOINT a Leader** to it.
- The Leader's **specialty/specialization determines the boost** for that research line: an economist boosts economy-line research, a horticulturist boosts agriculture-line, etc. Leaders have no universal bonus — their specialty defines where they help.
- **Surprise/breakthrough bonus (positive wildcard):** at research completion, there's a chance of a surprise bonus IF the appointed Leader's specialty aligns with the project. This is the *good* twin of the wildcard surprise-encounter — sometimes research turns out BETTER than the projected numbers.
- This makes research a PEOPLE decision (who is appointed) and unifies Codices + Leaders + research tree into one system.
- Interplay with Codices: research costs Codices (the knowledge/special resource) + time, gated/appointed through a Leader. Embers/chipsets feed deploy/domains (separate AI track).
