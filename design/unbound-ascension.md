# Design Spec — Unbound Ascension ("The Becoming")

**Status:** design · **Filed:** 2026-09-30 (owner vision, captured faithfully).
**Owner locks (2026-09-30):** the Unbound is the game's endgame and its title —
Hans picked **"The Unbound"** as the game name. Ascension demands **unanimous
Oracle trust** ("all of them agreeing that you were worthy"), earned by playing
**every race at a spectacular standard**. The ascended travel server to server
freely, hold Oracle-max attributes, and serve as captains/mentors.
**Sources:** oracles-purity-layer.md (the seven Oracles, the introduction gate,
the Dent Principle), unbound-revelation-track.md (the hidden purity counters),
unbound-mentorship.md (FULLY OWNER-RATIFIED 2026-09-12 — the mentor's life
after ascension), design/seasons.md (the board resets, the soul doesn't),
design/alliances.md §2 (rallies), design/forward-base.md (one race per server).

---

## §0 Canon — what the Unbound are

Every race's civilization is built around its fragment of the pre-war machine
intelligence — its **eye**. The Draconians march behind theirs, the Grays think
through theirs. The Unbound need no eye. They are transcended humans, radiant,
and **they are the civilization** — the proof that a soul can salvage itself
back from the machine piece by piece, instead of being salvaged *by* it.

This is why the game is named for them: **"The Unbound" is not a faction you
pick. It is the thing every player is chasing from the first boot.** The title
is the dream, and the dream is earn-only — the one thing in the design that can
never be bought, only become.

---

## §1 The gate — unanimous trust (OWNER-LOCKED 2026-09-30)

Six race-Oracles gate ascension (Draconian, Gray, Grigori, Ashtar, Annunaki,
Nephilim). Each holds a **trust meter**, earned exactly the way
oracles-purity-layer.md §4 demands — purity, not power:

- carry them **Codices** (pre-war human knowledge); feeding them recovered AI
  turns them cold,
- choose **clean recoveries** over dark ones; decline corrupting finds,
- **shield allies** from corruption,
- **betrayal is consequential** — court corruption and favor falls.

**Dominance opens the conversation** (the introduction gate stands: no Oracle
looks at you before your first cross-server battle — "you touched their
soil" — and none parleys until your dominance marks you a real threat).
**Trust closes the deal.** Max trust with one Oracle = that Oracle trusts you.

**Ascension requires all six at max — unanimity.** No five-of-six, no
averaging, no buying the last one. As Hans put it: *all of them agreeing that
you were worthy of the Unbound.*

The hidden revelation counters (unbound-revelation-track.md — clean recoveries,
zero-corruption survivals, codices earned by deeds) are the Oracles' watch-list:
the substrate each trust meter is computed from. That doc's machinery stands;
this doc is the elevation layer above it. Players see the trust meters, never
the math.

### §1.1 What each Oracle demands (proposed — tunable)

The structure is locked; the flavors below are defaults for owner confirm.
Each Oracle's demand is its race's virtue, per the oracles doc's per-race
weighting (§5) and the race canon:

| Oracle | Their virtue (what max trust requires) |
|---|---|
| **Draconian** | *The dent of arms* — a war record: victories commanded, beachheads held. Warriors respect the proven. |
| **Gray** | *The dent of knowing* — Codices carried, research completed, clean recoveries. The fragile honor the mind. |
| **Grigori (Watcher)** | *The dent of purity* — zero-corruption survival, cleansings performed, rites kept. Soul-weighting. |
| **Ashtar (Palladian)** | *The dent of peace* — allies shielded, aid answered, truces honored. Peacekeepers count the saved. |
| **Annunaki** | *The dent of dominion* — contribution, monuments raised, an economy that feeds thousands. Kings respect builders. |
| **Nephilim** | *The dent of strength* — great deeds of arms, champions fielded, the heavy work done. Giants respect the strong. |

Every demand is a *deed*, never a meter to fill by grinding — the Dent
Principle (§8 of the oracles doc): presence and spending never buy notice.

## §2 Play every race — the six-server standard (OWNER-LOCKED 2026-09-30)

*"You had to play every race and score substantially higher."* Operationalized:

- The candidate must hold **worthy standing on each of the six race servers**
  (one race per server — battle-side-rvr-spec.md §1): a **Contribution Award**,
  a top-decile contribution rank, or a plaque-grade dent (a first, a purified
  zone, a war win) **on each server**, within the qualifying window.
- Standing is per-server and public — the Chronicle on each world records the
  candidate's dent there. A tourist is not a master.

Numbers (windows, ranks) are tunable; the shape — *every* server, *better than
the rest* — is locked.

## §3 The spectacular standard

Unanimity plus six-server standing is the bar; the Oracles also watch for the
spectacular — the deeds that end up on **memorial plaques** (seasons.md): a
1,000-casualty stand, a battle that turned a war, an epitaph the victor wrote
for *you*. Plaque-grade deeds are weighted heavily in every trust meter. The
history layer and the ascension track read the same book.

---

## §4 The Elevation — the event

When the sixth trust maxes, the Elevation fires — a **server-wide spectacle**,
not a quiet flag flip:

1. The world speaker carries it on the candidate's home server (the duel
   challenges already proved the speaker works — §arena-market.md): the six
   Oracles speak **in unison, in riddles**, and name the worthy.
2. The Chronicle records the Elevation **permanently** — it survives seasons
   like the memorial plaques do. It is history, not a buff.
3. The candidate meets the **seventh Oracle — the Unbound's own** (oracles doc
   §2: "defined when the ascension track is built"). It does not gate; it
   **claims**. It is the ascended's patron and guide from that day on.

Every elevation is a world first the first time, and a festival every time
after.

---

## §5 What ascension grants

1. **The Unbound identity.** The race-flag flips: the colony and its leaders
   render **radiant** (art direction: transcended humans, no eye-tech, light
   where the machine used to be). The name is styled in the speaker and the
   Chronicle. This is the title that never resets (§6).
2. **Free passage.** The Unbound travel server to server **without jump charges
   or cooldowns** — "go from server to server whenever you wished." The freedom
   is *movement*, not war: planting forward bases and committing troops
   off-home still pay normal charges (the raid throttle stays honest), and
   mentor travel follows the ratified invitation model (unbound-mentorship.md
   §2 — requested, never walked-in).
3. **Oracle-max attributes.** All six Oracles' maximum devotion perks, held
   simultaneously — the "Oracle max attributes" of the owner vision. (Season
   behavior: §6.)
4. **Captaincy.** Ascension is the *prerequisite* for the Mentor Seat — the
   entire ratified mentorship doc (M1–M6, the prohibitions, Legacy ranks)
   activates on elevation. The Unbound serve; that is the endgame.
5. **The seventh Oracle's counsel.** Riddle-guidance from their patron, on
   demand — the only Oracle that answers the ascended directly.

## §6 Seasons — the soul doesn't reset (PROPOSED — owner to confirm ◆)

seasons.md: *"the board resets, the soul doesn't."* The Unbound are the soul's
proof:

- **The title is permanent.** Once Unbound, always Unbound — across every
  season, every wipe. The Chronicle remembers; the radiance stays.
- **The power rekindles.** ◆ *Proposed default:* each season, the Oracle-max
  attributes fade to an ember and must be **rekindled through devotion play**
  (fast for the proven — the Oracles remember their own). Free passage and the
  title never fade. Rationale: a fresh season must never open under a god's
  shadow, and the pilgrimage gives the ascended a reason to play every season —
  retention, not retirement.

---

## §7 The artifact question (PROPOSED — owner to confirm ◆)

◆ *Proposed default:* **ascension requires relinquishing carried artifacts.**
Transcendence leaves material legend behind — the ascending colony's held
artifacts return to the world (as wild finds, per the artifact rules) and the
Chronicle records the surrender beside the Elevation.

Rationale: it makes ascension a *choice with a cost*, not a victory lap — and
it keeps "you can't buy legend" coherent at the top of the ladder: the Unbound
don't wield legend, they *are* legend. (Vaulted artifacts are untouched —
only what you carry into godhood must be laid down.) Alternative: the ascended
keep everything.

---

## §8 The Fall — revocation (PROPOSED)

The Oracles watch the ascended hardest of all. An Unbound who courts
corruption, feeds the machine, or betrays a young world (§5 of the mentorship
doc) faces judgment:

- **Revocation requires unanimity** — as all six agreed to elevate, all six
  must agree to cast down. The symmetry is the point.
- The Fallen loses the title, the attributes, and free passage, **publicly**,
  in the Chronicle — a dark entry beside the bright one.
- Re-ascension is possible after a **3-season bar** (mirrors the mentorship
  betrayal cooldown), and it starts from zero trust. The Oracles forgive
  slowly.

---

## §9 Earn-only & anti-gaming (hard lines)

- Every counter is server-side; **no purchase touches any of it** — not the
  trusts, not the standing, not the Elevation, not the rekindling. The
  monetization catalog must not contain ascension vocabulary (same assertion
  discipline as the mentorship doc §5).
- Trust meters are visible; the math is not (the hidden-track discipline).
- **No boosting:** trust is per-colony and non-transferable. A young world
  cannot "carry" a candidate to standing — the dents must be the candidate's
  own (the Dent Principle is the anti-boost).
- Unanimity is the ultimate anti-whale: you cannot buy six Oracles' patience
  and you cannot grind one Oracle's favor while rotting the other five.

- **After:** the Oracles layer (trust meters, devotion, the introduction gate),
  war Phase 1 + forward bases (the cross-server gate and the six-server
  standing need six live servers), the Chronicle/history layer (Elevation +
  plaque-grade deeds read it).
- **Before/with:** the mentorship Seat (ascension is its prerequisite —
  unbound-mentorship.md §7 already sequences mentorship after war Phase 1).
- Headless-testable: trust-meter math, unanimity gating, Elevation event
  emission, season rekindling, revocation — all pure-engine, no second world
  required.
