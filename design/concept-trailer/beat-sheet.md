## Cut notes for the audio/motion pass

1. **VO pacing:** lines ≤ 6 s each; the Narrator stays low and deliberate, never rousing — the *music* carries the adrenaline (owner §11.4: "pump the heart").
2. **Sync anchors:** the four gold moments — decision window (3), claim chime (4), the bucket score (5), the Cradle ring (11) — are the *only* places the gold/purity sound plays. Celebration discipline, audio edition.
3. **The Chorus speaks once** (shot 6) — one line, distorted, no more. Every subsequent cut of it can be shorter; it never becomes a jingle.
4. **Silence budget:** exactly two silences — shot 9 under "What do we do?" and shot 10's fade. Nothing else goes quiet (per the upbeat direction).
5. **Captions:** every VO line ships as a caption string (WCAG AA), styled with the mono/ember chip language; the Chorus captions render in corrupt-magenta to match the voice (§5.3 label discipline — never color alone: prefix "CHORUS:").
6. **Unbound silence:** no shot names the track, shows its mechanics, or previews its heroes — only the *feeling* of "about to become everything" (shots 1, 12). The track's pull comes from the yearning, never the map.
7. **Honest labeling:** the assembled cut carries "TARGET RENDER — not gameplay capture" in the lower corner of every shot that is a still or mockup, per the deliverable's standing rule.
8. **Myth-fiction rail:** zero real-world religious/ethnic signifiers; the Keepers are armored scholar-commanders; the Chorus is machinery. Designers on the pass must reject any frame that reads otherwise.
9. **Per-race rollout (P8):** this sheet is the **Watchers** cut (violet identity, "The Vigil"); the other five races re-voice shot 4's Keeper line and swap the identity chrome via the six-world variables — one codebase, per-world constants, no new language.
