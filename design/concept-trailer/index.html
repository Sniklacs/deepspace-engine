Target render — not gameplay capture

Locked art direction

ASHED CIRCUITRY

A dead circuit-world, lit only by meaning — near-black blue-slate surfaces; every light on screen is a statement: ember-orange is human effort, magenta is the Chorus's grip, gold is the Cradle / Oracle / the return, and the six race accents color identity alone. The canon mood system (Blacksite Terminal base + Aurora Conduit layer + Foundry Amber as the ember/return state) taken to cinematic intensity.

surf-0 #070910 surf-1 #0b0f14 surf-2 #11161d ember #ff9d3c corrupt #e07cf0 purity #ffd166 watcher #b48cff hazard #4fd8c8

Palette

Canon tokens, verbatim (app.css/circuit-tokens.ts). Functional colors are global; race accents are identity; the two never cross.

Lighting

Motivated light in a dead world — every light means something. Volume, haze, ember particulates, filmic S-curve, vignette.

Mood

Three acts, one arc: triumphant (Height) → solemn & inevitable (Fall) → quiet, one gold spark (Wake). The Chorus is never beautiful.
