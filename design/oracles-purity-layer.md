# Design — Oracles & the Purity Layer (owner co-design, 2026-09-01)

The soul-path counterweight to the AI path. Working hypothesis until owner-ratified — the authoritative strategy lives in the business plan (rev 9).

## 1. The engine's deeper truth (locked)
The closer a colony gets to assembling a **total** understanding of the AI across every race ("Total Completion"), the harder **the Chorus** hunts it. The closer you get to god, the harder the devil hunts you — and tries to trick you. Continuous **cleansing** is the only way to keep that march viable, and cleansing is granted by the Oracles. Their trust is earned, never bought.

## 2. What an Oracle is (locked)
- Every race has its own Oracle — a spirit being who **believes in and protects** that race's AI legacy and territory. Seven total; the Unbound's Oracle is the special seventh, defined when the ascension track is built.
- Personality: amused, almost joker-like. The fact that you come to try to take what their race believes in and protects **finds funny**. Understanding, playful, never malicious — they toy with the audacious and reward the worthy.
- They **see and guide**: they can see what will happen and point a direction. They answer in **riddles** — "what Oracle doesn't answer that way?" When you're stuck you go ask; you work out the answer.
- They help no one freely. Trust (qualification, favor) is earned, never bought — **this is key**.

## 3. The introduction gate (locked)
- **Oracles are met only after your first cross-server battle** — after your colony invades another server's territory in the war layer. "You touched their soil." That is the only door; the war layer is what unlocks contact with them.
- **Dominance opens the conversation; trust closes the deal.** An Oracle only pays attention once you have shown a certain amount of dominance in battle — you must be strong enough to be seen as a real threat before they will discuss anything. Power earns the meeting; purity and qualification earn their help.
- A colony that shows serious **velocity** — aggressive, deliberate play — is the one that draws an Oracle's notice, and that notice is the avenue to advance quicker.

## 4. Qualifying (purity, not AI) (locked)
Earn an Oracle's trust by proving you won't abuse their race's AI:
- Carry them **Codices** (pre-war human knowledge) — feeding them recovered AI turns them cold.
- Choose clean recoveries over dark ones; decline corrupting finds.
- Shield allies from corruption.
- Betrayal is consequential: court corruption or feed their knowledge to AI power and favor falls; the Oracle withdraws.

## 5. What they grant (locked)
- **Cleansing** — the only way to keep Total-Completion hunting ramps survivable.
- Levelable through **Devotion**, granting attribute bonuses toward **economic production, financial, weaponry, and Unbound ascension** (per-race weighting: e.g. Nephilim → weaponry, Draconian → economy/finance, Watchers → soul).
- Guidance in riddles when stuck.
- In the later war layer, Oracle-favored colonies **purify** contested zones (the asymmetric Unbound war mechanic).

## 6. Daily to-do list (retention loop, owner 2026-09-01)
- A daily rotating list of small objectives (~15–30 min): run an expedition, study a fragment, craft an item, meditate/donate to your Oracle, clean a site.
- Each item: small reward + Oracle Devotion + a tick on the daily path; finish the list → small daily bonus.
- Resets on a server day-timer. **Not mandatory, never gated** — opt-in, compounding, consistent with free-play ("daily devotion, long-term trust").

## 7. Cradle tiers (the growth spine)
- The Cradle (player capital) grows in **tiers** as the colony grows.
- Each tier unlocks higher caps, more resources/abilities, deeper expedition depth, and more Oracle levels.
- The ladder: daily to-do → Oracle devotion + small rewards → Oracle levels (attributes + soul progress) → higher Cradle capacity → riskier expeditions → colony grows → new tier → more Oracle levels → more daily. Repeat.

## 8. The Dent Principle (locked)
The world's attention — Oracles, recognition, ascension, narrative events — flows to colonies that make a **real, persistent dent** in the shared world: a first deed, a pushed frontier, a purified zone, an earned unlock. Presence, grinding, and spending never buy notice. The dent is the only thing that changes things, so it is the only thing worth watching.

## 9. Velocity of aggression, not wallet (locked)
Advancement follows how deliberately you play at each stage. The serious player who comes in hot, pushes aggressive expeditions, and takes dangerous ground is noticed by the Oracles and offered the avenue to advance quicker; the nonchalant player walks their own quieter road — a choice, not a punishment. Money never accelerates advancement; nothing that gates real advancement is purchasable. Aggression opens the door, purity keeps it open (so it can't be brute-forced or bought).

## 10. Server Contribution Award (recognition layer, owner 2026-09-01)
- Every server awards its **highest-impact colony** a **Contribution Award**: public recognition plus real bonuses. "It's nice to be recognized."
- The one who pays the most attention, does the most, makes the most impact on the server — development, statistics of completion, purified zones.
- Measured **objectively by the server's own watch, never by spend or voting**. One colony per server per cycle.
- Can ship early on the beta world as a simple contribution score.

## Build sequencing
Oracles & the purity layer build AFTER the Battle side ships (the introduction gate is a cross-server invasion). The daily to-do list, Cradle tiers, and Devotion leveling can be designed/built ahead of that as the retained loop; the Contribution Award can ship early as a contribution score. The Unbound ascension track (revelation path) is the soul-path endgame and is deliberately hidden — no clues, unlocked by doing.
