# Fallen Hero Recovery

Owner decision: Hans, Oct 12 2026. Drafted same day from his locked notes; uncommitted, awaiting his word.

## Core rule
When a leader/hero goes down in battle, they drop their artifacts/gear on the field.
The hero is OUT OF SERVICE until recovered and healed.

## Extraction
- The commander must send an EXTRACTION CREW to recover the body — risking more troops to get them back.
- Keep reserve troops at the Cradle specifically for extraction.
- Veteran/elite troops are the extraction specialists.
- Success is a PERCENTAGE roll: the crew may recover the body but not the gear.
  More trained/experienced troops = better odds.

## The 5-minute window
- Get the extraction team in within 5 minutes or the loot goes to the other side 100%.
- After the window, it is a percentage chance you reclaim anything at all.
- ANYONE can claim a fallen hero — if you do not extract, rivals can take them.

## Recovery
- Recovery takes 24 hours once the hero is back.
- EXPEDITE: recovery can be sped up by purchasing from the store (monetization hook).

  while the cargo-only rule still governs ordinary troop/hero loot in battles without an extraction.
