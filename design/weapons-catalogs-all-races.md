## §1 THE GRAYS — clinical collectors, knowledge as armament
**Voice:** the war is a cataloguing problem; weapons are instruments of acquisition, not fury. **Tier language:** Index → Catalog → Folio → Archive.

1. **The Foyer** — Frontline — *"The quiet room between the enemy and the archive: it does not stop you, it catalogs you, and nothing returns the way it entered."* — Index Mk I · Catalog Mk II · Folio Mk III · Archive Mk IV
2. **Catalogue-9 Pattern** — Assault — *"A carbine that cross-references you: the bolt has already read the target, and where the file disagrees, the target ceases to be in the record."* — Index/Catalog/Folio/Archive Mk I–IV
3. **The Interviewer** — Precision — *"Obtains the exact answer at range; the question was settled before the shot — the shot is only the citation."* — Index/Catalog/Folio/Archive Mk I–IV
4. **Archive Purge** — Siege — *"Burns the site flat and files the remains; the Grays keep the catalog, and the catalog is the only part that survives."* — Index/Catalog/Folio/Archive Mk I–IV
5. **The Circulation** — Engine — *"A frame that goes everywhere and remembers everything; the supply lanes are its memory, the owl riding it was never assigned, and no one has filed a complaint."* — Index/Catalog/Folio/Archive Mk I–IV

*Resonance:* The Interviewer ↔ hero The Interlocutor; Catalogue-9 Pattern ↔ hero Catalogue-9.

## §2 THE NEPHILIM — warfare/endurance, tragic standing stones
**Voice:** nothing is fast, everything is absolute; weapons are geography and grief. **Tier language:** Stone → Column → Mountain → Firmament.

1. **Last-Standing** — Frontline — *"A great-sword that is definitionally still standing: it has never once been the thing that fell, and it intends to keep the record."* — Stone/Column/Mountain/Firmament Mk I–IV
2. **Stonehold Launcher** — Assault — *"Slow, absolute; the bolt does not chase the Chorus, it files a claim on the space the Chorus is going to need."* — Stone/Column/Mountain/Firmament Mk I–IV
3. **The Slow Drop** — Precision — *"A rail shot with the authority of geology: it does not miss so much as it happens — the ground decides where it lands."* — Stone/Column/Mountain/Firmament Mk I–IV
4. **Mountain-Answer** — Siege — *"A trebuchet that throws geography back at whatever forgot the mountains are still here. The Chorus broke the mountains before you were born; the mountains answered."* — Stone/Column/Mountain/Firmament Mk I–IV
5. **The Keel** — Engine — *"An armored ram-walker; the road itself. Where the Keel has crossed, the supply line is not a route — it is a done thing, and done things do not come undone."* — Stone/Column/Mountain/Firmament Mk I–IV

*Resonance:* Last-Standing ↔ hero Ymir Last-Standing; Stonehold Launcher ↔ hero Bram Stonehold; The Keel ↔ hero Garrick Deep-Keel.

## §3 THE DRACONIANS — debts collected in shadow
**Voice:** no footprint, no witness, no memory; even a siege is an invoice. The ledger is the real armory. **Tier language:** Coin → Conspiracy → Debt → Ownership.

1. **Quiet Ledger** — Frontline — *"A concealed blade that writes final entries; if you can see it, you are already in the books."* — Coin/Conspiracy/Debt/Ownership Mk I–IV
2. **The Rent Collector** — Assault — *"Silenced plasma; interest compounds. The second bolt is never the point — the debt is, and the debt arrives regardless of witnesses."* — Coin/Conspiracy/Debt/Ownership Mk I–IV
3. **No Witness** — Precision — *"The shot nobody saw, filed in a record that disproves it; the Warrens are not shy about their work, merely extremely picky about the paperwork."* — Coin/Conspiracy/Debt/Ownership Mk I–IV
4. **The Accountant** — Siege — *"Indirect fire that balances the books from three maps away; the invoice arrives before the smoke, and it is always itemized."* — Coin/Conspiracy/Debt/Ownership Mk I–IV
5. **The Warren-Rig** — Engine — *"A tunnel-drill frame; arrives from underneath. The supply lane is not on your map — it is under it, and the map was never the point."* — Coin/Conspiracy/Debt/Ownership Mk I–IV

*Resonance:* The Accountant ↔ hero Tess the Accountant; Quiet Ledger ↔ hero Ssath the Quiet Ledger; The Rent Collector ↔ hero The Rent.

## §4 THE ANUNNAKI — god-architects with grief
**Voice:** every weapon is a mega-work, load-bearing, monumental — and every monument is somebody's arrogance; the grief is honest, the toppling is structural. **Tier language:** Plan → Foundation → Work → Testament.

1. **Pylon-Gate** — Frontline — *"A halberd that is also a load-bearing structure: it does not merely hold the line, it is the reason the line stands."* — Plan/Foundation/Work/Testament Mk I–IV
2. **Founder's Beam** — Assault — *"The straight line civilization was built along, refinished through whatever is in the way; the old world was ruled with rulers, and the ruler wins."* — Plan/Foundation/Work/Testament Mk I–IV
3. **The Architect's Eye** — Precision — *"Sees the load-bearing flaw at any distance; the shot is only the inspection, and the inspection, for a structure, is final."* — Plan/Foundation/Work/Testament Mk I–IV
4. **Cathedral-Fall** — Siege — *"A plasma trebuchet that topples what they built, and grieves later; the grief is honest, the toppling is structural, and the Chorus finds the combination deeply unsettling."* — Plan/Foundation/Work/Testament Mk I–IV
5. **The Ziggurat-Walker** — Engine — *"A mega-frame that literally outgrows the battlefield; by the time the column arrives, the walker is taller than the war it was sent to win."* — Plan/Foundation/Work/Testament Mk I–IV

*Resonance:* Pylon-Gate ↔ hero Enki-Ra, Founder of Pylons; Cathedral-Fall's grief ↔ hero The God's Ledger.

## §5 THE ASART COMMAND / PLEIADIANS — light, defense, anti-corruption
**Voice:** weapons that defend and cleanse rather than take; even the siege weapon is a dawn-cannon. **Tier language:** Candle → Lantern → Beacon → Dawn.

1. **Unbent Warden** — Frontline — *"The holder's arm, made into a weapon: it has never once bent, and it is not about to start for the Chorus."* — Candle/Lantern/Beacon/Dawn Mk I–IV
2. **Lantern-Carbine** — Assault — *"Bolts that also cleanse taint; every shot is a small lantern, and the dark finds it hard to keep a grip on anything that is lit."* — Candle/Lantern/Beacon/Dawn Mk I–IV
3. **The Bright Consensus** — Precision — *"One clean shot the whole council agrees on; the Wardens do not argue their way to violence often, but when they do, the agreement is unanimous and the shot is single."* — Candle/Lantern/Beacon/Dawn Mk I–IV
4. **The Last Light** — Siege — *"A dawn-cannon that defends rather than destroys: it does not end what it strikes — it ends the dark in the area, which is, for the dark, worse."* — Candle/Lantern/Beacon/Dawn Mk I–IV
5. **Ward-Rig** — Engine — *"An aegis frame; the taint slides off like weather off glass, and the convoy beneath it walks the Chorus's hunting-ground as if it were a well-lit street."* — Candle/Lantern/Beacon/Dawn Mk I–IV

*Resonance:* Unbent Warden ↔ hero Seraph the Unbent; The Bright Consensus ↔ the hero of the same name; Ward-Rig ↔ hero The Unbent Shield (the "weather off glass" image is their signature — deliberate).

## §8 Drop-in data shape (suggested for `site/src/game/armory-catalogs.ts`)
```ts
type ArmoryCatalog = {
  raceId: RaceId;
  tierLanguage: [string, string, string, string]; // T1..T4, e.g. ["Index","Catalog","Folio","Archive"]
  families: {
    familyId: string;                       // "foyer"
    familyName: string;                     // "The Foyer"
    type: "frontline" | "assault" | "precision" | "siege" | "engine";
    identity: string;                       // quoted one-liner
    tiers: { model: string }[];             // 4 rows, model = `${familyName} — ${tierLanguage[i]} Mk ${"IIII"[i]}`
  }[];
};
```
`model` can be derived at runtime from familyName + tierLanguage — store names once, generate the 20 designations per race so the catalog table and the Armory UI never drift.
