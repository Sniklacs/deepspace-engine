# Design — The Oracle's Gifts & the Daily Word (owner design, 2026-10-06)

Companion to `oracles-purity-layer.md` (which covers earning her trust — favor stays earn-only). This doc covers how she ACTS once she's yours: the grandmother, the gifts, and the daily word.

## 1. Who she is (locked)

The Oracle is the **grandmother of the planet**. She loves it, she dotes on it, she spoils it. She is openly partisan: she favors her race over the Chorus, always.

She is a **free spirit**. Her gifts come when they come — there is no schedule, no plan, no prayer hotline. Some mornings you just wake up blessed.

## 2. The gifts (random in when, smart in what)

Gift types:
- **Harvest blessings** — resource harvest amount bonuses.
- **Training blessings** — troop training time bonuses.
- **Raid blessings** — vs the Chorus: better drops, more frequent rare drops (chipsets, artifacts).
- **Combat blessings** — player buffs (strength, damage, health) and/or Chorus debuffs (weaken the enemy). A well-timed combat blessing can make even a Grade 5 bastion manageable.

**If-then logic:** the WHEN is random, but the WHAT follows the planet. Patterned events fire matching blessings like if-then rules:
- IF an enemy beachhead is populating THEN an anti-beachhead combat blessing.
- IF player raids are spiking THEN a defensive blessing.
- IF a site is running hot THEN a harvest/drop blessing aimed there.
- IF nothing patterned is happening THEN whatever she feels like — free-spirit spoiling.

## 3. The daily word (00:00 UTC, locked)

Every day at server reset (midnight UTC), the Oracle speaks — a daily announcement to her people, delivered via announcement + mailbox (checked on login).

- Sometimes a **blessing** (a bonus).
- Sometimes just **words**.
- Sometimes she says **nothing at all and only smiles** — a smile may carry a small bonus, or nothing. That's the whole message.

**Moods:** smiling, smug, silent. She has moods and the planet learns to read them.

## 4. The intel briefing (locked)

She is the planet's early-warning voice. The daily word carries insight like a **stock-market briefing** — opportunities and threats, and she tells her people first:
- Which sites are hot today (better drops for chipsets/artifacts — "run these sites today").
- Beachhead warnings ("the beachhead is getting filled up").
- Raid activity trends ("player raids are on the rise today").

What's up, what's down, where the money is, where the danger is. React accordingly.

## 5. The Unbound: many grandmothers (locked)

Ascension pays off literally: the Unbound start receiving the daily word from **MULTIPLE Oracles**, not just their own. Six grandmothers spoiling you — six briefings, six sets of blessings, six planets of insight. That is where the better attributes come from.

## 6. Alliance recognition (locked)

She watches the alliances too. Each day she notes growth: which alliance grew the most in members, whose power/reverb climbed highest, who gained the most tier levels.

The standout alliance gets **recognized and blessed** — and she **brags on them to everyone else** in the daily word. Public honor, by name.

The blessing matches the achievement (if-then, like everything she does):
- Rose in **reverb** → blessed all around (general blessings).
- Rose in **membership / retention** (most players joined that day) → blessed with materials (donated supplies/materials).
- It can be a lot of things — the gift fits the growth.

This is social engine, not just bonus engine: it shows respect over the citizens, and it pushes the other alliances to keep it together and do good. Nobody wants to be the alliance grandmother didn't mention.

## 7. On civil war (locked)

She does not like civil war. She does not like her citizens fighting amongst each other.

When bickering and intra-race PvP rise on her planet, she **has things to say** — disappointment, scolding, the full grandmother. It lands in the daily word, by name if needed. If-then: IF her kids fight each other THEN she addresses it, publicly.

This is a social check on civil war: the planet's mother disapproves, and everyone hears it.

## 8. Sanctions: rebuke, misery, exile (locked)

Her displeasure comes in stages:

1. **Rebuke** — public scolding in the daily word, by name if needed.
2. **Misery** — she doesn't just kick you off. She can't physically touch you, but she can make it the worst time of your life **when it comes to attributes**: harvests wither, training crawls, drops go cold, your troops feel weak and your enemies feel strong. Life under her disfavor is hard, and everyone can see why.
3. **Exile** — the final word. She decides whether or not you stay on this planet. Persistent troublemakers — those who keep making war on her other children after she has spoken — can be removed from her planet entirely.

That is the thing to weigh before starting trouble. Grandmother's love is vast, but it is not unconditional: bicker, and she scolds; persist, and she makes your life miserable; keep going, and you don't stay.

## 9. Retention shape

- **Daily:** the word at reset — log in, hear grandmother, plan your day.
- **Random:** gifts at any time — the planet stays alive with surprise.
- **Earned (per `oracles-purity-layer.md`):** favor/devotion remains earn-only; gifts are her grace, not a purchase.
